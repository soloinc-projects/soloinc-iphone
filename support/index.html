Support

Write to support@8086.llc. Say which iPhone and iOS version you use, and which version of SoloINC your Mac runs.

First questions

What do I need?
SoloINC on a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 26 or later (a free download from SoloINC for Mac), and an iPhone on iOS 18 or later. The iPhone is the Mac's remote; nothing runs on the phone.
How do I pair the iPhone with my Mac?
On your Mac, open SoloINC and choose Pair a phone. It shows a code that works once, for ten minutes. In the app, tap "Pair with my Mac" and scan it, or paste it.
The app says my Mac is out of reach.
Check that SoloINC is open on the Mac and that both are on your own network, then tap Try again. Until then the app shows the last copy with its age, and every action is off; nothing is queued.
Can I use it without a Mac?
Not in this version. Without a paired Mac the app does nothing.
Why did an ask reach me late?
While the app is closed it checks for new asks at most every 15 minutes, when iOS allows. Opening the app, or pulling down on Today, checks at once.
Why did several asks come as one notification?
Asks from an agent your company marks low risk, coming within 15 minutes, are grouped. Each is still its own answer. Turn grouping off, or change its window, in Settings › Notifications.
How do I answer from the Lock Screen?
Touch and hold the notification, tap its answer (or Decline, which only puts the note away), and confirm the answer with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. Turn it off in Settings › Notifications.
How do I stop everything quickly?
In the app, tap Stop on the Desk. Outside it, add the Stop everything control to Control Center or the Lock Screen. Either way your Mac does the stopping and writes it to the record, and you can start again.
Does the app cost anything?
No. It is free and sells nothing; your SoloINC on the Mac holds the plan.
How do I unpair or remove my data?
Settings › Unpair this iPhone. Deleting the app removes everything it kept on the iPhone. See the privacy policy.
